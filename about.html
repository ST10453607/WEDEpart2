    • About Paws & Home

    Founded in 2015 by a group of local volunteers, Paws & Home Animal Shelter has grown into a Non-Profit Organisation dedicated to animal welfare in our community.

    Our Vision

    To build a community where there is always a home waiting for an animal in need.

    Our Mission

    To rescue, rehabilitate and rehome a stray or abandoned cats or dogs, while providing basic veterinary care such as vaccinations and sterilisations.

    Who we work with

    We work with local families looking to adopt, generous donours, dedicated volunteers and local vets and pet shops who support our mission.
